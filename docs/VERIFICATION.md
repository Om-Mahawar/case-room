## What was checked

On 12 Nov 2026 Claude re-read each of the 16 draft skeletons against the column-corrected text of its casebook pages and checked, line by line:

- every entry in the fact sheet is something the **interviewer** says in the source, with the same figure;
- every figure the **candidate** proposed sits under accepted assumptions, not facts;
- the arithmetic in every quant moment, recomputed;
- the crux, clue and close against how the source case actually turns;
- each open verifier point, with a decision recorded.

## Result

No factual error was found in the 16 drafts. 34 verifier points were settled; the decisions are below and in each file's `verifierFlags`. Four changes were made on the way: the Vistara case was reset as an unnamed airline and run as a drill, with the book's unsupported ₹30,000 ceiling dropped; the post-merger case's ambiguous revenue line was fixed to one reading; the cough-syrup company is "mid-sized" only; and interviewer probes from the transcripts were added to several cases.

## What this check is not

It is a second pass by the same model that drafted the skeletons, not an independent check by a person who has interviewed. It can confirm that a skeleton matches its page. It cannot confirm that the crux is what a real interviewer would test, or that the anchors separate a 7 from a 9 the way a human would. The specification's human check (about 15 minutes a case) and the calibration run (T-CAL) are still due before students rely on the scores. Until then every case is labelled **AI-checked**.
